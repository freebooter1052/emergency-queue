Heap benchmark. Same generated operation stream for every structure; every ExtractMin is
checked against a reference ordered set. Educational priorities, fictional patients.
Per-operation ns come from a calibrated CPU cycle counter (timer overhead subtracted); 'touches/op' counts heap entries or nodes read/written
(a memory-traffic proxy, NOT hardware cache misses). Largest deterioration drop: 200 points.
