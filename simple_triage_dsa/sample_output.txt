
=============== PREDEFINED TRIAGE DEMONSTRATION ===============
All patients are fictional. The priority formula is a teaching example,
not a validated medical triage formula. Lower score = attended earlier.
Zone capacities:  zone 1 = 2  zone 2 = 3  zone 3 = 3  zone 4 = 2  zone 5 = 2

------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: Patients arrive in two waves (heap insert, O(log n) each)
------------------------------------------------------------------
First wave arrives at minute 0:
  Added patient 101 (Asha Menon), ESI 3, deterioration 0, zone 2 -> priority score 200
  Added patient 102 (Ben Carter), ESI 4, deterioration 0, zone 3 -> priority score 300
  Added patient 103 (Chen Li), ESI 2, deterioration 0, zone 1 -> priority score 100

30 minutes pass before the second wave arrives:
  Advanced simulated time by 30 min for 3 waiting patient(s).
    Patient 103: wait now 30 min, score 100 -> 40
    Patient 101: wait now 30 min, score 200 -> 140
    Patient 102: wait now 30 min, score 300 -> 240
  Cost: all n scores changed, so the heap did O(n) work (n updates + one
  bottom-up rebuild) and the segment tree did 5 point updates, O(k log k).

Second wave arrives at minute 30:
  Added patient 104 (Diya Rao), ESI 3, deterioration 0, zone 2 -> priority score 200
  Added patient 105 (Ethan Brooks), ESI 5, deterioration 0, zone 4 -> priority score 400
  Added patient 106 (Farah Khan), ESI 3, deterioration 1, zone 2 -> priority score 170
  Added patient 107 (Gabriel Ortiz), ESI 4, deterioration 0, zone 3 -> priority score 300
